It is advised from Rome of the 10th of June, that Cardinal Corradini had thirty votes in one of the scrutinies, so that he wanted only five to be chosen Pope, and that in the next scrutiny he had thirty two votes, insomuch that he wanted only three; and that it was the general opinion that he would be elected. Ibid.

They write from Vienna, that the heavy artillery was set out for Iyaly, and that they continued to send thither all sorts of ammunition of war; and that these circumstances, join’d to the little haste the Emperor shews to return an answer to the last proposals of the Allies of Seville, made them fear an approaching rupture: They add, that the Emperor had assured the Great Duke, that he will never depart from the Quadruple Alliance, and that this Prince has promised powerfully to assist his Imperial Majesty with money and provisions. Ibid.
